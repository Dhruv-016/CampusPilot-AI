# CampusPilot AI — Synthetic Student Handbook for Academic Demonstration

> This handbook is fictional. It is NOT an official university document and has no legal authority.

## 1. Fee Payment Procedures
Semester fees are paid through the Student Payment Portal using UPI, net banking or card. After a successful payment the portal issues a transaction reference. Payments normally reflect on the student fee record within 2 working days. Students should keep the transaction reference until the fee record shows the payment.

## 2. Fee Payment Deadlines
Semester fees must be paid in full by the published due date for that semester. For Semester 3 of the demonstration year the due date is 15 November 2026. Payments made after the due date attract a late fee of INR 500 per week. Partial payments are accepted, but the full outstanding amount must be cleared by the due date to avoid the late fee.

## 3. Payment Portal Problems
If the Student Payment Portal fails during a payment, do not pay repeatedly. Note the failed-transaction reference and a screenshot of the error. Check your bank statement and the fee record after 2 working days. If the amount was debited but is not reflected, email the Fees Office at fees@campuspilot.example with the transaction reference. If the portal outage is confirmed in the IT outage log and the failure happened on the due date, the Fees Office extends the deadline to the next working day for that student, and no late fee applies. Without a confirmed outage, the normal due date applies.

## 4. Working-Day Terminology
A working day means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays and university closure days listed in the academic calendar. Saturdays and Sundays are not working days. "Next working day" means the first working day after the date in question.

## 5. Academic Calendar Guidance
The academic calendar lists semester start and end dates, examination weeks, fee deadlines and the course change window. Semester 3 begins 1 September 2026 and ends 18 December 2026. Mid-semester examination week begins 2 November 2026. Semester 4 begins 11 January 2027. Students should check the calendar at the start of each month. Dates may change only through an official notice from the Academic Office.

## 6. Student Support Contacts
Fees Office: fees@campuspilot.example. Academic Office: academics@campuspilot.example. Student Help Desk: helpdesk@campuspilot.example, open on working days from 9:30 to 17:00. Scholarship Cell: scholarships@campuspilot.example. Contact the office that matches the topic of your question.

## 7. Scholarship Enquiries
Scholarship enquiries are handled by the Scholarship Cell through the Student Help Desk. The university provides merit-based scholarships for students maintaining a CGPA of 8.5 or higher and need-based financial aid. Students should provide their student ID and the name of the scholarship scheme. Applications must be submitted through the Scholarship Cell within the first two weeks of the semester with academic transcripts or income documentation.

## 8. Course Change Enquiries
Students can find information about courses, course catalogs, syllabi, prerequisites, and registration on the Student Academic Portal. Course changes, additions, and drops are allowed only during the course change window published in the academic calendar. For Semester 3 the window closes on 26 November 2026. Course change requests must be approved by the programme coordinator.

## 9. General Policy Enquiries
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each course; attendance records are reviewed periodically and medical leave requires a certificate within 3 working days. Examinations require a valid student ID card and hall ticket. CampusPilot AI operates on an agentic LangGraph pipeline with regex redaction, zero-retention memory, and local auditing. For matters not covered here, students should contact the Student Help Desk.
